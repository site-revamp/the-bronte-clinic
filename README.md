# The Bronte Clinic Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

The Bronte Clinic is a doctor-led medical aesthetics and dermatology clinic established in 2017 by Dr Fiona McCarthy. With clinic locations in London and Guildford, it provides regenerative aesthetics, advanced skin treatments, cosmetic procedures and specialist dermatological care.

The redesigned homepage focuses on strengthening clinical credibility, clarifying the patient journey and making treatment enquiries and location-specific bookings easier. Its direction reflects the clinic's emphasis on medical expertise, personalised treatment planning and natural-looking results.

## 2. Main Improvements

The redesign addresses three priority problems identified during the website review.

### 1. Clearer Clinic Location and Clinical Credentials
- Identified the conflicting London clinic addresses displayed across the website and external records.
- Improved the presentation of clinic location information, with address details requiring final verification before publication.
- Clarified the distinction between CQC registration and inspection or rating status.
- Avoided presenting registration as evidence of a positive inspection outcome.

### 2. Improved Booking Journey
- Replaced reliance on generic booking messages with clearer consultation and enquiry pathways.
- Improved the distinction between London and Guildford clinic booking options.
- Made the next steps in arranging a consultation easier to understand.
- Strengthened the visibility of appointment-related calls to action.

### 3. Improved Content Quality and Treatment Information
- Addressed grammatical issues, including correcting "supported robust clinical data" to "supported by robust clinical evidence".
- Improved the organisation of treatment information and patient-focused content.
- Created clearer pathways to treatment details, practitioner information and relevant patient concerns.
- Improved the presentation of contact information and treatment navigation.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual presentation:** Strengthened the professional medical aesthetics positioning through clearer content presentation.
- **Layout:** Improved the hierarchy of clinic information, treatment categories, practitioner details and booking sections.
- **User experience:** Made treatment discovery and consultation enquiries more straightforward.
- **Navigation:** Improved access to treatment information and location-specific booking pathways.
- **Content consistency:** Addressed grammatical errors and improved the presentation of clinical and business information.
- **Trust and credibility:** Gave greater importance to practitioner expertise, clinical credentials and accurate regulatory information.
- **Overall appearance:** Created a more structured and professional homepage aligned with the clinic's doctor-led approach.

## 4. Implementation Notes

Before publication, confirm the correct patient-facing London clinic address and ensure all location references, map links and contact details are consistent. CQC registration and inspection status must be represented accurately. Treatment descriptions, practitioner credentials and booking links should also be verified before going live.
